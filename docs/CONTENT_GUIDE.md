# Content Guide

> Status: Skeleton. Content guidelines for the bilingual platform.

## Languages

- Indonesian (primary market).
- English (international learners and employers).

Every learner-facing piece of content must be available in both languages. The
foundation must not assume a single language.

## Demo vs Official Certification

- Demo assessments are for practice/validation only.
- Demo assessments must never be presented as official certification examinations.
- Any UI copy or labeling must clearly distinguish demo content from official
  certification (which is future work).

## Tone

- Clear, encouraging, and technically accurate.
- Consistent terminology per language.
- Avoid ambiguous or untranslated technical jargon where a good localized term exists.

## Content Review

Content guidelines will be expanded as course and lesson milestones begin.
